COLD CALL SCRIPT
Source: Appt Setting Module

Works for cold leads, Facebook/ad leads, warm leads - exact same structure. Takes a cold lead who barely knows you and turns them into a warm booking.

BEFORE YOU DIAL:
- Be in the right state of mind
- Stand up. Execute with body language even though they can't see you. It translates into tonality subconsciously. Lying down = flat energy. Standing, performing = conviction and energy.
- On the phones it's 100% tonality - but body language drives tonality

THE PAPER TRICK:
Have a printed document in front of you - something you can rustle/shuffle audibly. Paper crunching is a pattern interrupt. It signals: "I have something physical in front of me related to you." They immediately think: what is that? It breaks the standard cold call pattern.

---

OPENING:

[Rustle paper]
"Yeah, hey - is this... is this [First Name] [Last Name]?"
[Confirming identity, slightly like you're checking a list]

"Oh hey [Name] - it's just [Your Name] from [Company]. Look, I can't talk for long, I've actually got another call lined up in a couple of minutes. I was just wondering - can you help me out for a moment?"

WHY: "I can't talk long" = relief (not going to be trapped for 10 minutes). "Can you help me out?" = humans instinctively want to help. Buys you the seconds you need.

If they say "who's this?":
"Yeah, it's just [Name] from [Company]."
[Downplay it - casual, like it's nothing]

"Not sure if you speak to your neighbours much - I've actually got the government funding notice here in front of me for your area there at [their street]."
[Rustle the paper]
"I just finished helping out [NEIGHBOUR NAME] a couple blocks over from you the other day. Do you know them at all? [Two random specific details about the neighbour - restaurant owner, new baby, little dog, whatever]"

WHY: Pattern interrupt. They're thinking "do I know them?" Not thinking "this is a salesperson." You've bought more seconds.

---

TRANSITION:

If they don't know the neighbour:
"Oh no stress at all. Yeah, look - I just called because we've gone through this notice here..."
[Rustle paper]
"...especially on page two."
[Rustle paper again]
"I just had a quick question for you - you guys are already on solar over there, hey? Or no."

NOT: "Do you have solar?" - they've heard that 500,000 times.
YES: "You guys are already on solar over there, hey? Or no." - sounds like you're checking, not pitching.

They say no:
"Oh totally understand - I get it, probably just moved in and been busy with everything. And just so you know upfront - I'm not really calling to sell you anything today at all."
[Chuckle - genuine, casual]

WHY: You've been tonally different from every other cold caller up to this point. Now you confirm it. They believe you because your whole energy has backed it up.

"But there is a genuine reason I gave you a buzz. Let me explain quickly."
[Rustle paper]

---

THE PITCH (identical to D2D from here):

"There's been a massive [rebate/government incentive] that's just come out for [location] and a lot of people are honestly still unaware of it."
[Drop volume slightly, draw them in]
"It's completely changed how solar works."
[Certain, slight pause]

"What we're doing now - you can actually put a system on the roof for literally zero cost out of pocket at all. And the way it works is actually pretty simple."

"We put a big enough system and battery on the roof so it covers your entire electricity bill. Your bill ends up at zero - you'll probably be sitting on a little bit of credit. And instead of paying that money to your power company every quarter, you just redirect it to pay off the system. Does that concept make sense?"

[Yes]

"Yeah, exactly. And as you can imagine, everyone's doing it - because the bills, they just don't stop going up, do they."
[Playful, obvious - talking to a mate]

"At least this way, the repayments stay locked in at the same amount every month. And most of the time it's actually less than what you currently pay on power. So nothing out of pocket. It's pretty much just like owning your home instead of renting it - same money going out the door each month, but now it's building equity in something that's yours."

If not fully bought in - keep going in the same casual, genuine energy. Never shift to sales mode.

MINDSET REFRAME (burning house analogy - Alex Hormozi):
If someone was in a burning house, upstairs, in the window - would you hesitate to help them? No. You'd want to help them. Same energy here. You genuinely see that they're in a bad situation (overpaying for power) and there's a much better option. You're not pushing a product - you're trying to help a friend.

---

QUALIFYING THE BILL (take it away):

"To be honest though - one thing I should mention - you might not even qualify."
[Casual concern]

"What are you actually getting stung for on power lately? Like what's your average bill roughly per quarter, off the top of your head?"

Under minimum threshold: "Yeah, look - I'll be straight with you. At that level it's actually not going to be the most sensible for you."
[Try to get a referral anyway]

Over minimum: "Wow - you're getting smashed."
[Shocked, concerned]
"I'll for sure get a specialist to help you. Mark my words - I'll get someone out to help you."

Right around average: "Yeah, that's what we normally see for families who qualify for the right amount of funding."

---

BOOKING THE CALL:

"I mean, would you be against us running you through the numbers to even see if it makes complete sense for you? Worst case, we spend 20-30 minutes helping you get a bit more clarity on your situation. Best case, we can potentially help you save... I mean, thousands even."
[Pause. Let them respond.]

From here: identical to the D2D script.
- Works well, doing lots of installs in area
- Promo / case study urgency
- Need both partners on the call (savings guarantee condition)
- Two options for day/time (never open-ended "when are you free?")
- Final confirm / check calendar / send confirmation
- Send company resource/case studies before the appointment

---

FOR FACEBOOK / AD LEADS:

Swap the opening:
"Hey, is this [Name]? Oh hey - it's just [Name] from [Company]. Look, I can't talk for long, I've got another call lined up in a couple minutes. But I saw that you put your name down - I think you might have clicked on an ad on Facebook for us. Does that ring a bell?"

Whatever they say - build off it. Doesn't matter what the answer is.

"Yeah, look - by the way, not sure if you speak to your neighbours much..."
[Transition straight into the neighbour script from there - identical from this point]

---

OBJECTIONS: Identical to D2D script objection handling.

"Just leave a flyer / send info":
"Yeah fair enough. Honestly if it was a flyer worth of info I'd have sent it over and been on my way. The thing is the numbers are just different for every house - your roof, your bill, your power use. Can't really tell you what you actually qualify for from that. Worst case we get some clarity, best case hopefully save you thousands. Completely up to you though."

"Not interested":
"Yeah totally fair. Has your bill been creeping up recently too though?"
[Childlike curiosity - re-engage, don't fight it]

"Need to think / talk to partner":
"100% - that's honestly why we don't make any big decisions on the phone. The walkthrough is just for both of you to sit down together, see the numbers, and decide as a household. Does that make sense?"

"Can't afford it":
"Yeah that's the most common thing I hear - which is exactly why this rebate exists. No upfront cost as long as you qualify, and repayments end up less than what you're already paying on power. You're not adding a bill, just redirecting what's already going out the door."

---

WHY THIS SCRIPT WORKS:
Standard cold calling = numbers game, only easy sales convert.
This script = every qualified prospect gets intrigued because it's a pattern interrupt. It's different from everything they've heard. When executed correctly, you should feel like you CAN book every qualified person you speak to.

Key differentiators:
1. Paper rustle = pattern interrupt, creates physical credibility
2. "Can't talk long" = immediate relief
3. "Can you help me out?" = flips dynamic
4. Neighbour reference = not a random cold caller
5. "Not here to sell anything" = said only after tonality has already proven it
6. Casual, friend-to-friend energy throughout = never sounds like a pitch
