DOOR-TO-DOOR SCRIPT
Source: Appt Setting Module

The best door-to-door script for solar. Used to break every door-to-door record. It stops being a numbers game - every qualified person you speak to, you should feel like you WILL book them.

GENERAL PRINCIPLES:
- Speak slowly. Be calm. Be confident.
- Come across as someone worth a homeowner's time
- Be quick on your feet - make natural jokes (dog, something you notice, anything)
- You're here to genuinely help, not to force a booking
- What to wear: NO suit. High-vis or working shirt. Casual trade look. Lower sales resistance threshold immediately.
- Tonalities are key - every line has an intended tonality (curious, concerned, playful, casual, certain)
- 93-94% of human communication is tonality and body language

THE SHEET:
Carry a sheet. One side: current government funding/rebate info for your area. Other side: example of a bill in credit (zero dollar bill). This is your prop throughout the conversation.

---

OPENING AT THE DOOR:

Knock, knock, knock.

[Look down at your sheet as they open - curious tonality]
"Hey, are you guys the... you guys are homeowners here?"
[Confused, curious, uptone - like you're checking something]

"I can't stay for long - I've just got an appointment in a couple of minutes literally just on the road. But I was wondering if I could quickly get your help for a sec."

[Hand on heart - casual, friendly]
"I'm just [name] from [company], by the way."

[So casual - you know me, I'm just the friendly neighbourhood guy]

"Not sure if you speak to your neighbours much - I just finished helping out [NEIGHBOUR'S NAME]..."

[CRUCIAL: Always get a neighbour's name before this door. Even if you don't get a sale, shake their hand at the end and ask their name. Use it here.]

[Turn and point in the direction of that neighbour's house - body angle open, side-on, not straight at them]
"...just a couple blocks over. Do you know them? [Specific detail about the neighbour - blue roof, little puppy, etc.]"

[Why: Buying time. They're thinking "do I know them?" If yes - great. If no - no problem. Keep moving.]

---

THE TRANSITION:

If they don't know the neighbour:
"Oh no stress at all. Well, hey - I was actually going to ask your roof... Just curious, how come you guys haven't got solar yet?"
[Curious + slightly concerned tonality - like you're genuinely wondering why they're missing out]

Whatever they answer - acknowledge it:
"Yeah, yeah, totally understand. I know, probably just busy, everything like that."

[Hands up, chuckle]
"And just so you know upfront - hands up - I'm not here to sell anything today at all."
[Chuckle - casual, relaxed. They relax immediately.]

"But there is a genuine reason I'm here. I'll show you - lean in."

[Hand them the GOVERNMENT FUNDING SIDE of the sheet]

As they read:
"There's been a massive [rebate/government incentive] that's just come out..."
[Curious tonality]
"...and a lot of people are actually still unaware of it."
[Slightly concerned/surprised]
"It's completely changed how households work."
[Certain, hands gesture to emphasise, look them in the eye]

---

EXPLAINING THE CONCEPT:

"So what we're doing now - you can actually put a system on the roof for literally zero cost out of pocket at all. And the way it works is pretty simple."

[Flip the sheet in their hand to the ZERO DOLLAR BILL side - collaborative move, lean in together]

"We put a big enough system and battery on the roof so it covers the entire electricity bill."
[Circle the zero dollar amount with your finger]
"So your bill ends up looking like that."
[Point at the credit amount]
"You're actually going to have a little bit of credit here as well."

"Instead of paying that money to the power company every quarter - you just sort of redirect it."
[Hand gesture - physically show the redirect motion]

"Does that kind of make sense?"
[They say yes]

"Yeah, exactly. And you can imagine everyone's doing it - because the bills just don't stop going up, do they."
[Obvious, playful - like you're talking to a mate]

"At least this way, the repayments stay locked in at the same amount every month. And most of the time it's actually less than what you're already paying for power. So it doesn't even cost you anything out of pocket. It's pretty much just like owning a home instead of renting - you're going from paying a liability to an asset."

---

EXTRA BUYING POINTS (if they're not fully bought in yet):

Blackouts:
"And honestly, the bigger reason a lot of families are doing it right now - you know with all the blackouts we've been getting lately? You're completely protected. Lights stay on, fridge stays on, everything keeps running. Everyone else on the street is dark - but not you. You're not at the mercy of the grid anymore."

---

QUALIFYING THE BILL:

[Take it away - create slight scarcity]
"To be honest though - one thing I should mention - you might not even qualify."

[Genuinely curious]
"What are you actually getting stung for on power lately? Like, what's your average bill running per quarter, off the top of your head?"

If UNDER your minimum threshold (e.g. under $350/quarter):
"Yeah, look - I'll be straight with you. At that level, it's actually not going to be sensible for you to do this."
[Still try to get a referral: "Is there anyone else around here we might be able to help?"]

If OVER minimum threshold:
"Wow - you're getting smashed."
[Shocked, concerned - like this needs immediate attention]
"I promise you I'll get someone out to help you."

If right around average:
"Yeah, that's what we normally see for families. That's right around the range where we can get all the government funding."

---

ASKING FOR THE BOOKING:

"Well, what most families are doing is - like I said - just switching that over."
[Hands physically gesture the switch]
"So you're not forced to pay those bills as well. No, they just keep going up."

"I mean, would you be against us just running you through the numbers to even see if it makes complete sense for you? Worst case, we spend 20-30 minutes helping you get a bit more clarity on your situation. Best case, we can potentially help you save... who knows, probably thousands."
[Pause. Let them respond.]
[Casual, playful, zero pressure]

THE PROMO / URGENCY:
"Works well though - we're already doing a lot of installs in the area. So if we're able to speak to more people while we're here for the next couple of weeks, it just makes it a lot more affordable for us. We're actually covering [first 3 months of payments / whatever your promo is] - so you're nothing out of pocket."

GETTING BOTH PARTNERS:
"One of the only companies in [location] that will guarantee your savings from solar. So if you went solar with us and didn't save as much as what we showed you, we'd actually be liable to pay you the entire difference. As you can imagine, that's a big liability for us - so the only condition is that we need to speak to both you and your partner together in the walkthrough."

BOOKING THE TIME:
[Look down at phone - expect a response]
"Is early next week or later next week better for you?"

NEVER say "what day are you free?" - always give TWO OPTIONS. Basic human psychology - easier to pick from options than to generate a solution.

FINAL CONFIRM:
"Just to check - you guys don't plan on moving in the next couple of years, hey?"
[If yes, moving soon: pitch home value increase / up to you if you still sit them]

After booking:
"Beautiful. I've just sent the confirmation to your phone and email now. Did that come through? Just want to double check - we've been having a couple of issues with bookings not coming through properly."
[Gets them to look at it = higher show rate]
"Sweet - just pop that into your calendar when you get a sec. We're pretty smashed at the moment with the current funding, but if anything comes up, no stress - just let us know and we'll work around you."

Always send them a resource link: case studies / company website / background on the company. Prospects who come to the presentation knowing your company are 100x easier to close.

---

OBJECTION HANDLING:

"Just leave a flyer / send info through":
"Yeah, fair enough - makes sense why you'd want a flyer. Honestly, if it was a flyer worth of info I'd have handed it over and been on my way. The thing is, the numbers are just different for every house - your roof, your bill, your power use. A flyer really can't tell you what you actually qualify for. That's why we get a specialist to walk you through it properly. No pressure at all - worst case you get a bit more clarity, best case we hopefully save you thousands. Everyone around here is doing it. Probably wouldn't be the worst use of your time - but completely up to you."
[Then just chill. Look around. Don't wait desperately for their response. You don't need it - and they can feel that.]

"Not interested":
"Yeah, totally fair. From what [neighbour name] told me, the bills around here have been climbing pretty hard recently. Has yours been creeping up too?"
[Childlike curiosity - just re-engage them in conversation]
If they answer - qualify on the bill and you're back on script.
If still not interested - don't fight it. Ask a question and try to re-spark interest. Can't force a mindset shift at the door.

"Need to think about it / talk with my partner":
"100% - and that's honestly why we don't make any big decisions or anything crazy on the door. The walkthrough is just for both of you to sit down together with a specialist, see the numbers, and decide as a household. Does that make sense?"
[They agree - keep going. You just agreed with their objection and turned it into a reason to book.]

"Can't afford it right now":
"Yeah, that's totally fair - and honestly, that's the most common thing I hear. Which is exactly why this rebate exists. There's no upfront cost as long as you qualify - and the repayments end up less than what you're already paying on power every month. So you're not adding a bill, you're just redirecting what you're already losing to the grid every month. Does that make sense?"

---

MINDSET NOTES:
- Never be desperate. You're not staking your whole day on this one booking.
- You have a solution that benefits them. If they don't want it, no problem - there are plenty of other people who will.
- Stay in the power hand at all times through calm certainty and genuine care.
- The casual energy, the chuckle, the playfulness - that's not an act. You genuinely enjoy helping people and you're confident in what you've got.
