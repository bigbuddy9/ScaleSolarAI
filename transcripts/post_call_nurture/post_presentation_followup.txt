POST-PRESENTATION FOLLOW-UP
Source: Post Call Nurture Module

WHY THIS MATTERS:
If you only rely on one-call closes, you're leaving a massive amount of revenue on the table. Follow-ups are a separate revenue stream. The best reps get consistent follow-up sales every single week on top of their one-call closes. Like an NBA player only focusing on assists - what about the rebounds, the two-point shot, the three-point shot? Be a complete salesperson.

This sequence is based on what worked as the #1 rep at a $100M/year company, breaking national records consistently.

THE HIGH-INTENT FOLLOW-UP (set this on the call before they leave):

After you've objection-handled a couple of times and can tell they're not going to close today:

"Yeah, I completely appreciate that [name]. To be honest, I'm pretty similar myself - I don't like making big decisions on the spot either. I would be pretty under the pump at the moment, as you can imagine, with the current government funding. So let me squeeze in a quick 30 [minutes] on either [Day X] or [Day X+1] - just to go over everything in detail, answer anything that's come up for you over the next couple of days, just to help you guys out. If that sounds fair, let me know which day works better."

RULES FOR SETTING THE FOLLOW-UP:
- Maximum timeframe: day after tomorrow. Not "I'll call you in a couple weeks."
- Always give TWO specific day options - never open-ended
- Frame it as helping them, not chasing them
- You're picking a date, not asking if they want one

Never say: "Oh yeah, I'll give you guys a buzz in a couple of days." That means nothing. They'll ghost you.

THE MANAGER EXTENSION (if they can't do your proposed times):

Don't just accept a vague "we'll figure it out." Use the manager close:
"Look, I want to make sure you don't lose this. I can't just hold the offer for the next five weeks. Let me buzz my manager and see what I can do for you."

This makes the follow-up feel like something they need to show up to - not just a casual chat. It's predicated on something real being held for them.

AFTER THE CALL - SAME NIGHT:

1. Send a professional summary email covering:
   - What you went through together
   - The key numbers (their bill, the system cost, the repayments, the savings)
   - The offer/case study that's been reserved for them
   - Next steps and the follow-up time you've locked in

2. Text them the same night - casual, personal, start a real relationship:
   - Keep it chill, not corporate
   - Something genuine, not a template-sounding message

3. GROUP CHAT TIP: Add both partners (husband and wife / both decision makers) to a group text with you. Loop both into all emails as well.
   Why: You can get sales where only one partner shows on the follow-up call and says "we've already made the decision, we're good to go." When both are looped in through the whole process, they discuss it together between calls. Dramatically increases conversion on follow-ups.

TOUCHPOINTS BETWEEN BOOKING AND FOLLOW-UP CALL:

Don't overdo it (they'll feel chased) but don't go dark (they'll forget everything).
- Night of the call: text + email
- Day before follow-up: quick touch (SMS)
- Day of follow-up: reminder

The templates and exact timing are in the SOP document. Use them exactly.

ON THE FOLLOW-UP CALL:

If they're happy to proceed: go straight into closing (order form → finance).
If they need more time: exact scripting is in the SOP. Re-anchor the urgency, re-set another high-intent follow-up date.
If they don't show: specific re-engagement script in the SOP.

CORE PRINCIPLES:
- Never let a follow-up be vague - always a specific date and time
- Both partners always looped in
- Keep touching base enough that they stay warm, not so much they feel pressured
- The email after the call is a professional recap that does the selling for you overnight
- Use the manager close to make the follow-up feel like something real is being held for them
- Follow-ups are not chasing - they're serving people who need a bit more time to make a good decision

Follow this procedure and you'll have a consistent flow of follow-up sales on top of your one-call closes every single week.
