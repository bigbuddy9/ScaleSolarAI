POST-SALE COMMUNICATION
Source: Post Call Nurture Module

WHY THIS MATTERS:
If you don't do this, you'll get cancellations, fall-throughs, and customers who don't trust you and won't refer you. The sale is not the close - it's the start of the relationship that earns the next one.

"The sale isn't the close. It's the start of the relationship that earns the next one."

Referrals are a separate revenue channel on top of one-call closes and follow-ups. Companies doing $100M+ a year are built heavily on referral pipelines. This is the process that builds that.

IMMEDIATE POST-SALE (buyer's remorse window):

As soon as someone signs, they get buyer's remorse. That's psychology - it happens to everyone. You need to address it immediately.

Pillar 12 (the reaffirm step at the end of the presentation) is the start. But post-sale communication continues that job for the weeks until installation.

Goals:
- They feel constantly in the loop
- They feel taken care of
- They feel proud of and happy with their decision
- They don't cancel

THE POST-SALE SEQUENCE:

Both the rep AND admin should reach out consistently. If no admin, the rep does it all.

1. WELCOME CALL (same day or next day after signing):
   - Welcome them, confirm they're good
   - If finance wasn't completed on the call - follow up and get it done
   - Confirm next steps and timeline

2. FINANCE FOLLOW-UP (if not done on call):
   - Use the authoritarian/urgent framing from Pillar 11
   - Make it clear this has to be done to lock in the deal
   - Don't let it drag - 50% of finance not completed on the call will fall over

3. ONGOING CHECK-INS (between signing and installation):
   - Solar installations can take 2-6 weeks
   - Keep them in the loop the entire time
   - Any pre-site approval / designer visit - communicate clearly what's happening and when
   - They should never be wondering what's happening next

4. INSTALL DATE:
   - Make sure the install team looks after them
   - Monitor that it goes smoothly

5. POST-INSTALL CALL (call them personally the day of or day after installation):

Script:
"Hey [first name], it's [Jesse] from [company]. Do you got a sec?"
[Yes]
"We just wanted to give you a quick call now that the install's all wrapped up. How'd it go? They look after you?"
[They say yes / smooth sailing]
"Perfect. Awesome. Look, before I let you go - these things genuinely live and die on word of mouth. If you know anyone - family, neighbour, anyone who's been talking about getting solar or battery, even just thinking about it - I'd really appreciate the intro. We look after anyone who's sent our way. No pressure at all, I just want to put it out there. Cheers [first name] - enjoy the system. I'll be in touch in a week or so to make sure everything's running sweet."

Alternative framing:
"If you want someone to have the same level of service we gave you - if you want to refer us, we're happy to help out. We're trying to help as many people as possible."

If they don't answer: leave a voicemail or send a text with the same message.

6. 30-DAY CHECK-IN:
   - How's the system running?
   - Any questions on the app or monitoring?
   - Referral ask again if not already actioned

7. 6-MONTH CHECK-IN:
   - Are the savings coming through as expected?
   - Any issues to address?
   - Referral ask

The more consistently you check in with a genuine process, the more referrals you build over time. This compounds. Month after month, you build a stream that runs alongside everything else.

KEY PRINCIPLES:
- Never let them feel forgotten between signing and install
- Personal touch beats automated messages every time
- The referral ask is always casual, zero pressure, genuine
- Both partners looped in on all communication
- The goal of every touchpoint: they feel proud of the decision and want to tell people about it

This is how you go from a good sales rep to a complete revenue engine. One-call closes + follow-ups + referrals + marketing = the full picture.
