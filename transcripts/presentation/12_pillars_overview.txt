THE 12 PILLARS — SCALE SOLAR PRESENTATION OVERVIEW

The presentation is 12 pillars in sequence. Each builds on the last. Reps can ask about any pillar by name for the full detail — this is the map of the whole flow.

Pillar 1 — Build Rapport. Get them to genuinely like you and see you as a real human, not a salesperson. Without rapport there is a 0% chance of the sale. 2-5 mins upfront, then maintained across all 12 pillars. Rapport = flexibility to push back, challenge, and hold frame.

Pillar 2 — Solar Explanation. Establish yourself as the expert. Educate them simply and confidently so they trust you. Authority compounds into every later pillar. Pre-handles "I can't afford it" (Green Loan) and "what if I sell my home?"

Pillar 3 — Solar Industry Issues. Make cheap feel dangerous. Get them genuinely concerned about dodgy installers and bad systems. One of the most important pillars — if you get a price or shop-around objection at the end, you didn't execute this well. Tonality is genuine concern, not attacking competitors.

Pillar 4 — Company Credibility. Pillar 3 made them anxious; Pillar 4 makes you the answer. "If I'm doing solar, this is the company I'm doing it with." A release pillar — confident, relaxed, matter-of-fact. Proof speaks for itself, no bragging.

Pillar 5 — Why Our Products. Confirm the gear is the real deal, no cheap corners. A tick-the-box, lowest-sales pillar. Educator mode, not pitch mode. Cashes in the quality promise from Pillar 4.

Pillar 6 — System Design. Make them feel the system was built specifically for their home, bill, and future. "They've actually thought about this." Sets the bespoke anchor that every later number relies on. Pre-handles "can't I go smaller?"

Pillar 7 — Guarantees and Warranties. No scenario where they're exposed — every angle covered for 25 years. Six protections walked confidently. The biggest separator from cheap competition and justifies the price gap. Sets up the savings reveal as legally guaranteed.

Pillar 8 — Power Bill Pain. The only pain anchor in the presentation. Let them feel the cost of doing nothing — rates, total spend, 25-year projected cost. Direct, knowing, "can you believe this" energy. Build it, let it land, don't labour it. Sets up Pillar 9 as relief.

Pillar 9 — Solar Savings Breakdown. Walk the math for their exact home. Intentionally lands solar-only as modest — the battery is held back for Pillar 10. Calm, meticulous, let the numbers do the work. Structural contrast does the selling.

Pillar 10 — Case Study Close. THE close. The longest and most important pillar. The case study mechanism unlocks the discount (earned, not begged). The aha reveal: monthly payment LESS than their current power bill. Honest close — "you guys happy with that?" instead of "are you ready to move forward?" Honesty as method, slow and clear.

Pillar 11 — Finance Walkthrough. Operational, not philosophical. Get the product form signed AND the finance app completed (or committed within 24 hours) before the call ends. Half of customers who don't finish the app on the call fall over. Calm, assumptive, hand-hold every step. Lead, don't ask.

Pillar 12 — Final Steps. Deal is done — stop selling, start the 25-year partnership. Reaffirm the decision, kill buyer's remorse, pre-frame the next-day admin call. Warm, sincere, not a victory lap. Leave them feeling they gained a partner, not that they got sold to.
