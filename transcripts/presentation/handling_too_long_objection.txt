Handling the "This Is Taking Too Long" Objection During the Presentation

When a prospect says the presentation is taking too long or asks you to skip to the price, do NOT just jump straight to the price. First, justify why the full presentation matters.

Script - Justify the Process:
"Guys, this is extremely important. This is a 25-year investment. It's a 25-year relationship. We're taking you through this so you can completely understand and be informed on a decision that could potentially save you $100,000 over that time. I want to be cognizant of your time and respectful of it, but don't think I'm going through all this fluff for no reason - it is extremely crucial."

Then get buy-in to continue:
"I can definitely speed it up. Can I keep going so you have everything you need to make a fully informed decision?"

If they say keep going, continue with the presentation.

Reversing the Order (Hard Cases):
Sometimes you can read the room and know the prospect will not be open to any conversation until they see the price. In those cases, you can reverse the order:
1. Jump to the price first
2. Walk them through the savings breakdown
3. Then go back and complete the rest of the presentation

The key is critical thinking - read the individual. Some people need the context first, others need the number first before they'll engage. You have to figure out which type you're dealing with.

What NOT to do:
- Do not blindly skip to price without any justification
- Do not ask them to evaluate price without context (price without savings = resistance)
- If you do show price early, immediately follow with: "Now let's look at what this system will actually do for you in terms of savings" - never let them sit on the number alone

The Line When They Say It's Taking Too Long:
"Totally fair - let me condense this and get straight to the numbers so you can see the cost and what it actually does for you in savings."

Then show price confidently, don't engage on it yet, and immediately say: "Now let's look at what this system will actually do for you in terms of savings."
