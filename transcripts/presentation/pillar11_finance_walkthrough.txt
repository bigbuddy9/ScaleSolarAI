PILLAR 11: Finance Walkthrough
Source: Presentation Modules

Probably the simplest pillar. You've built everything in the first 10 pillars. This is just leading them through the finance and making sure it actually gets done on the call.

THE #1 RISK HERE: 50% of finance deals where the application doesn't get completed on the call will fall over. They open it, never finish it, and the sale dies. You've put in all the work - don't let it fall over here.

GOAL: They complete the finance application before they get off the call. Not "they'll do it later." Done. Now.

FRAME IT AS AUTHORITARIAN AND URGENT:

"Guys, there's a few really important things I need you to do for me right now."

NOT: "Could you do it if you have a second..."
YES: "I need you to do this for me right now."

"Once these are done, we take care of everything else. I just need you to work with me for the next 15 minutes or so."

Tie it to the case study close - they need to get the finance done to lock in the deal and the offer you just presented. The limited case study offer is predicated on getting the finance done now.

STEPS:

1. Product Form - get something signed first (agreement/product form). Handle this via screen share on Zoom or in person - walk them through it step by step.

2. Finance Application - walk them through every single step. Don't leave them with a task. Do it with them, live, right now.
   - Steps vary depending on your finance provider (Parker Lane, Brighte, etc.)
   - Whatever the steps are - guide them through each one
   - Don't move to the next step until the current one is done

KEY MINDSET FOR THIS PILLAR:
- Be the leader. They follow your direction.
- Stress the importance of completing it now
- Use the urgency from the case study close - the special offer needs to be locked in
- If you don't hold their hand through the finance, they'll get distracted, put it off, and the sale falls over

That's it. Nice and easy. Just finish what you started.
