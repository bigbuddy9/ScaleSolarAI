PILLAR 12: Final Steps
Source: Presentation Modules

You've got the sale. Finance application is done (or if they had to leave early, you've made it crystal clear they must complete it by the next day - as covered in Pillar 11).

GOAL: They leave the call feeling 100% proud of their decision, with zero buyer's remorse, and clear on what happens next.

WHY THIS MATTERS:
Buyer's remorse is psychology - everyone feels it immediately after making a big decision. You need to handle it straight away before it sets in. Don't skip this just because you've got the sale.

HOW TO DO IT:

Be warm, genuine, not salesy. Make it feel like a partnership and a real relationship. You're genuinely happy for them.

Key things to say:
- "Genuinely congrats - you're going to save heaps of money over the next 25 years."
- "I'm really happy I could help out. I love seeing people save this much money."
- "You're completely protected. I'm so glad you picked us because we really do care about you - and you'll see how much we take care of you."
- "If you ever need anything from me, let me know. I'll always be available."

Walk them through the next steps:
- What happens after the call
- What the installation process looks like
- What they can expect from here
[Reference your presentation's next steps slide for the exact process]

Personal sign-off. Make it genuine. Make them feel like they've started a real relationship with you and the company - not just completed a transaction.

EXECUTION NOTES:
- Do not skip this - you've done the whole presentation, got the sale, there's zero reason not to double down and affirm the decision
- Short and warm - doesn't need to be long
- Leave them feeling excited, not second-guessing
