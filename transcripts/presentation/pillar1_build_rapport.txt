Building rapport leverage:
- They feel bad picking another company over you
- They feel bad cancelling after the sale
- They'll respond to your texts and calls
- They'll actually tell you the truth and be able to identify what the real objection is on the call
- They'll genuinely think you're a nice person
- They'll be happy for you to get the sale

How to get into rapport immediately:

1. CHILDLIKE CURIOSITY - Do not jump straight into the presentation. Be human. Actually care. If they tell you something interesting, probe on it, ask, show you're actually interested. You're not just a random robot who just wants a sale.

2. MANUFACTURE FAMILIARITY - Almost like a cheat code to get into immediate rapport. For example: if you're European and see a European name, ask where they're from. If they're a new parent and you've got kids, connect off that. If they like a specific sports team, bring up the league naturally. These ways get you directly into rapport.

3. HUMANS MIRROR HUMANS - At the start of every call, your energy needs to be open and free. On a Zoom call: "Hey guys, how are you?" - raise your hands, smile. Why? In human psychology, when you raise your hands, a human immediately identifies there's no danger, so they bring their guard down. You're smiling because humans mirror humans - they're going to smile back immediately. That makes them think: "hey, this is a nice person, I'm enjoying my time."

Body language cues: hands up, open, slow pace, smiling. Don't speak at the speed of light - keep it calm.

RAPPORT QUESTIONS - always have 4-5 questions you execute every time:

Question 1 - Find their intention (ask with genuine curiosity):
"By the way, what was it specifically that [setter's name] mentioned to you on the door that, you know, got you thinking, you know, probably not the worst idea to have a chat with us - just so I know what piqued your interest, so we can have the most productive convo for you guys."

Question 2 - Industry awareness (uptone, genuine curiosity):
"Are you aware that the industry is a bit dodgy?"

The key is tonality - if you speak in monotone they'll turn off immediately. Pick out 5-6 questions you want to keep coming back to every time, develop your process.

PRE-FRAME (execute word for word once you're in rapport):
"Guys, what we'll chat about - we'll go through some formalities, you know, initial info, a bit about the incentives, most importantly we'll have a look at your current situation with the power companies, what that's actually looking like now, and what it's going to look like over the next 5, 10, 15 years or so. And we'll take a look at maybe where you want to be instead. And if you feel like what we chat about makes sense for your family long-term - like for example if you're saving an extra 100 bucks a month or something crazy - we're happy to show you the next potential steps. But honestly guys, that's pretty much it. I'm chill, so don't worry if you have any questions or anything, feel free to shoot, and I'll answer as best as I can. But yeah, that's it guys, we'll jump into it now. That all sounds helpful?"

What are they saying yes to? They're saying yes to: if we jump to the end and it makes sense, they're happy to go ahead. Subconsciously they've just admitted that to themselves.

After rapport, transition to Pillar 3 (Solar Industry Issues). You'll ongoingly build rapport as you go - if you see little remarks that make them laugh or crack the ice, use it. Ask questions throughout so they're not just listening to you the whole time.
