PILLAR 5: Why Our Products
Source: Presentation Modules

GOAL: They come out of this pillar completely sure they are getting the best quality products possible and would feel comfortable having them on their property.

Transition into this pillar with energy:
"Alright guys, let's jump into some of the nitty gritty. We'll run through the products, then the design, and then you get to see what our technicians have actually put together for you, and then the numbers."
- They lean back in immediately. Interest perks up again.

Keep answers high impact - bang, bang, bang. Don't ramble technical jargon they'll lose. They just need to retain the key information.

PANELS - Longi Solar (strongly recommended):

"Guys, I'm not here to confuse you with any technical jargon. I'm here to keep it simple. The panels we use are Longi. Let me walk you through why."

Key facts to rattle off quickly:
- World's largest solar panel manufacturer
- 10-20 billion dollars revenue every year
- Operating worldwide, been around since early 2000s
- First manufacturer to ever ship at scale
- Installed on FIFA World Cups, Apple headquarters
- Sleek all-black panels - not the ugly blue with grid lines like old panels

Tie back to Surf Club Standard:
"Remember how I mentioned we use the same products on the Surf Clubs? That product is the Longi panels."

THE 88.9% QUESTION (always ask this):
"Quick question for you guys - after 25 full years on the roof, in the sun, in the rain, in the heat, all of it - how much percentage do you reckon the panels would still be operating at after 25 years? What do you think?"

They'll guess low - 50%, 30%, whatever. The answer is 88.9% (Longi X6 family degradation rate).

"It shocks them every time. The technology is vastly improved. You put it on there and you're still going to be reaping the benefits 25, 30 years in the future."

[If using different panels - substitute in the actual degradation rate for that panel]

What they do is take the top 10-20 solar panels globally, test them under different extreme conditions - heat, shade, salt, wind, everything - and rank them from top to worst. The Longi panels have won every single year for nine consecutive years. Number one panel in the world. They are genuinely number one."

LLOYDS OF LONDON - the home run:

"The biggest thing for me - the reason I'm honestly such a huge fan of Longi - is they have a third party insurance policy with Lloyds of London. Lloyds are the oldest and largest insurer in the world. What that means is even in the absolute worst case scenario - if our company somehow goes out of business AND Longi also somehow goes out of business at the same time - that one in a thousand chance - guess what? You're still completely fine. You're still covered by Lloyds. There's no scenario where you're not protected. You'll get all your panel warranties covered no matter what."

Leverage this throughout the rest of the call whenever protection/warranty comes up.
