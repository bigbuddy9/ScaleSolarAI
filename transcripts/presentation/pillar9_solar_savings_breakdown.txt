PILLAR 9: Solar Savings Breakdown
Source: Presentation Modules

This is where you actually get the sale. Pillars 9 and 10 are the two most crucial closing pillars.

GOAL: The math has been done specifically for their home. They understand the solar numbers clearly. When the battery reveal comes (Pillar 10), they don't need to re-understand anything - they just need to decide if it makes sense.

TRANSITION IN (from Pillar 8 power bill pain):
Move straight from the power cost table down to their custom solar system in the calculator.

BEAT 1 - Build the Custom System in the Calculator:

Re-establish it's personalised:
"Guys, what's your postcode? We just need it because it calculates the exact sun hours per day. As you know, everything we do here has to be very meticulous."

Enter the system details from their design:
"Remember - we've got the 440W Longi panels. Let's look at your design together."
Count panels per roof face with them watching:
"On the north we've got 14, on the east we've got 5, on the west we've got 8."
Enter tilt angle:
"Do you know roughly what tilt your roof is at? Is it just sort of an average roof?"
[Check on Google Maps if needed. Note: tilt affects production - 30 degrees vs flat can make a meaningful difference. For Northern Hemisphere - logic is flipped: flat gets more production on south-facing roofs.]

BEAT 2 - Daily Usage (set this up carefully):

Re-anchor their usage number:
"Guys, do you remember - round about what was your average daily usage? Was it around 26 kilowatts?"
[Get them to re-confirm - checks they're still engaged]

Self-use split (solar only, no battery):
"We're going to look at just solar first. I might have something regarding the battery - so let's run through all the numbers with solar first."

"How much power do you reckon you use during the day?"

IMPORTANT: You want them to say a LOW number. Cap it at 50/50 maximum. Why? If you blow out the solar numbers too high, the battery contrast won't land as well.

Guide them to the right split:
- If both work during the day, out of the house: "Probably more like 30-70 then?"
- Typical household: "40-60 is what you normally see"
- Absolute max you accept: 50-50

Then calculate:
"Okay so 40% of 28 kilowatts is 11.2."
Enter 11 as the self-use figure.
"What we've just done - we've popped in the 40% of your power you'll be using during the day, because that's what the solar is going to cover. The rest of it, without a battery, you wouldn't be able to capture. Does that make sense?"

BEAT 3 - Enter Their Rates:

"So we had that 41 cent rate plus GST - so 45 cents per kilowatt hour."
Enter their exact rates. Feed-in tariff: 0.5 cents average if they don't have one.

BEAT 4 - Show the Production Chart:

"Guys, remember how we said your usage was going to be 28 kilowatts a day? Look at this - even in the lowest months of the year, the system covers you. Just like we showed you in the design."

Then paint the summer picture:
"And you know what's going to happen during the hotter months - January, February, December, most of the year outside of winter? You're actually going to have extra power. You'll be selling it back to the grid, making a little bit of extra profit. Your bill's going to be in credit."
[Show an example of a bill in credit on screen]
"This is probably what your bill is going to end up looking like. Now keep in mind - we can't factor all this extra credit into the calculations. But that's a little added bonus you're going to get."

BEAT 5 - The Costs and Government Funding:

Run through all line items:
- Panels: $X
- Inverter: $X
- All components
- Total system value: $X

Then the government funding:
"Even just for solar, we're at a really good time - you've got $1,300 of government funding coming off your system right now."

Build urgency:
"As I mentioned though - I can't guarantee that if you come back two or three months from now that's going to be the same. I've had customers who wanted to take a sec, pushed it off a couple months, came back upset that the price jumped up. That's not me doing anything - that's just the nature of the government incentives."

BEAT 6 - The ROI Breakdown:

"Don't plug into the payment plan yet" - go straight to the ROI graph first.

Walk through:
- "In your first year, you're getting an 8% ROI immediately."
- "And the thing about solar is that ROI goes up every single year - because it's offsetting the power increases. You're actually hedging yourself against inflation."
- "Guys, just from solar alone you'd save $2,477 off your bill. How much was your bill? About $4,000 a year."
- [Note internally: That gap is what the battery will fill - hold this for Pillar 10]
- "Even with just solar - over 10 years you're saving $34,000. That's a 110% return on investment."
- Flip back to power cost: "As opposed to spending $57,000 on power. At least you've got this option."

Make sure they understand:
- What the ROI graph means
- What the estimated savings figure means
- How the payback period works ("After about year X, you're getting your money back and you've got the next 15 years free")

BEAT 7 - The Cash or Green Loan Question:

"Hey guys - just suppose you were going to go ahead with this, would you be looking more at doing cash or using the green loan?"

No wrong answer - both are perfect:
- Cash: "Honestly, that's the best way to do it."
- Green loan: "Yeah, that's the best way. That's how everyone does it - because you pay nothing out of pocket."
- Don't know / unsure: Default to green loan.

If green loan - sell the loan provider's key points:
- No early exit fees
- No penalties for paying off early
- Fixed/stable interest rate
- Whatever the key points are for your specific provider (Parker Lane for Australia, or equivalent)

BEAT 8 - Show the Green Loan Numbers:

"Guys, you can see in your first year, you're out of pocket about $1,054 from the green loan. That's not too bad - and you can see you're still saving X amount after X years."

Then set up the transition:
"But remember - our goal was to make it so you paid nothing out of pocket at all. That's what we're going to need the batteries for."

TRANSITION TO PILLAR 10:
"You guys ready to jump into the batteries?"
[Yes - they're keen]

IMPORTANT: Before moving to Pillar 10 -
- If in person: leave laptop on the green loan screen
- If on Zoom: pause the screen on the green loan numbers
- When you come back and update to include the battery, the contrast will be much more dramatic and impactful

EXECUTION NOTES:
- Check in constantly - they need to understand every figure before you move forward
- The whole point of solar-only is to set up the battery contrast in Pillar 10
- Never let them get confused - if they don't understand a number, stop and explain it
- The self-use split is a key lever - keep it at 30-50% max, never higher
- Always re-reference their specific numbers (usage, bill amount, rates) throughout
- The moment they're clear on everything and keen to see the battery numbers - that's your green light for Pillar 10
