TONALITY BY PILLAR — QUICK REFERENCE

Every pillar has its own tonality. This is the cheat-sheet for how to sound in each one.

Pillar 1 — Build Rapport: Childlike curiosity. Genuine, human, no sales energy, no agenda. You're actually interested in the person, not ticking a box.

Pillar 2 — Solar Explanation: Confident and certain, explain everything simply. Like explaining something interesting to a mate, not delivering a pitch. "I'm not here to confuse you, I'm here to keep it simple."

Pillar 3 — Solar Industry Issues: Genuinely concerned, NOT pitching. Make-or-break tonality. Honest mate giving a real warning out of care — not a salesperson attacking competitors. Pace slows down. "I tell you this because I've actually seen it happen."

Pillar 4 — Company Credibility: Confident, relaxed, matter-of-fact. The release after Pillar 3's heaviness. No bragging, no hype — the proof speaks for itself, you just frame it.

Pillar 5 — Why Our Products: Calm, educator-mode, high impact. The least sales-y pillar. Like a knowledgeable friend showing you their car. No urgency — specs land harder delivered with confidence, not energy.

Pillar 6 — System Design: Light on the setup slides, confident on the design. Let the visuals carry the setup. Once you hit the actual design, shift to confident, calm, crisp — it was built specifically for them.

Pillar 7 — Guarantees and Warranties: Confident, calm, no hype — just walk the facts. Said like reading off a list of facts they're lucky to hear. Calm pacing, each guarantee gets its own moment. Tie every one back to "no stress."

Pillar 8 — Power Bill Pain: Direct, knowing, "can you believe this" energy. The one pillar you're most direct. A friend telling them they're in a bad spot — light chuckles and headshakes, not manufactured outrage. Build the pain, let it land, don't labour it.

Pillar 9 — Solar Savings Breakdown: Confident, meticulous, let the numbers do the work. Numbers-driven — calm and accurate beats hype. The calmer and more matter-of-fact, the more legitimate the numbers feel.

Pillar 10 — Case Study Close: Slow, clear, honest — honesty as method. The slowness is the proof. You're not selling, you're carefully walking them through a real opportunity with constant check-ins. The honest moments are what make it land.

Pillar 11 — Finance Walkthrough: Calm, assumptive, hand-holding — lead, don't ask. They're cognitively exhausted post-decision and want to be told what's next. "Here's what we're doing now, work with me." Assumptive, not asking.

Pillar 12 — Final Steps: Warm, sincere, partnership-start energy — not a victory lap. The moment you stop being a salesperson and become a partner. Real human warmth, not a closing technique, not a victory speech.
