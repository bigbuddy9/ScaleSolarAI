ACTIVE LISTENING
Source: Sales Basics Module

This one is less about being taught and more about training yourself to actually listen when the customer is speaking.

THE MISTAKE:
Customer tells you something meaningful. You move straight to the next line of your script with a completely irrelevant question. They notice. They think: he didn't listen. He doesn't care about me. Sales resistance goes up immediately.

THE SKILL:
When they say something - actually address it. Reference it back. Show it landed.

Example (general):
They've mentioned their mother a couple of times in conversation.
You say: "I can tell your mother means a lot to you." Then ask a question.
Their reaction: "Wow - he actually listened. He picked that up."

Example (solar specific):
They've mentioned the repayments a couple of times.
"Hey, I can tell the repayments are something you really want clarity on. I'm going to make sure we go over everything in detail so you're 100% certain."
Their reaction: gratitude, trust, lowered resistance.

WHY IT BUILDS RAPPORT:
Active listening is what makes a conversation feel real. If someone gives you context, tells you things about their life, raises a concern - and you just move past it - you've hurt the sale. They feel like a number, not a person.

If you actually listen, reference what they said, and engage with it - they feel seen. That's what builds genuine rapport.

HOW TO GET BETTER AT IT:
Know your script cold. When you're not using mental energy to remember what comes next, you have full capacity to actually listen to what the customer is saying. This is why scripting and practice matter - not so you sound robotic, but so you're free to be present.

Dive into interesting things they share. You don't need to go massively off track - even 10-15 seconds of genuine engagement with something they said builds enormous trust.

The result: more genuine conversations, higher conversion, more referrals.
