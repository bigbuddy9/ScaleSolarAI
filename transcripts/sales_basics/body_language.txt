BODY LANGUAGE
Source: Sales Basics Module

Body language pairs with tonality to make communication fully land. It's not something you force - it has to be fluid and natural or it looks strange and undermines trust.

THE CORE PRINCIPLE:
Use body language when it's natural. Don't manufacture movements. When it's forced, prospects notice and it reads as inauthentic. When it's fluid, it reinforces everything you're saying.

KEY MOMENTS TO USE BODY LANGUAGE:

The redirect gesture:
"We're just switching it from this... to that."
[Hand gesture physically showing the redirect - moving from one side to the other]
Simple, visual, makes the concept immediately tangible. One of the easiest and most effective uses.

The comparison gesture:
"It's less than that."
[Hold one hand higher representing their current bill, lower hand representing the repayment]
Visual contrast lands harder than words alone.

THE OPEN PALMS MOVE (resistance-breaker):
When you sense resistance or tension - raise your hands with open palms facing them.

Why it works: human psychology. When people see open palms, the brain reads "no danger." Sales resistance drops immediately. You can deploy this at any point in a conversation where the energy tightens. It's one of the simplest and most powerful moves you have.

ZOOM/PHONE CALLS:
Body language still matters even when they can't see your full body. Sit up or stand. Use hand gestures near the camera. Your energy and posture translate directly into your voice and tonality. A rep lying down sounds different from a rep standing up performing. Always perform as if they're in the room.

HOW TO IMPROVE:
- Start using it consciously in everyday conversations
- Watch how confident communicators use their hands and posture
- Don't try to master it all at once - just start integrating it naturally
- As it becomes habit, it compounds with your communication and tonality skills

The goal is fluid, confident, natural body language that makes your words hit harder. Not theatrics. Just presence.
