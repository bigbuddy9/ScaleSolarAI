COMMUNICATION
Source: Sales Basics Module

The first module of Sales Basics - and it's first for a reason. Without high-level communication, everything else fails. No respect from prospects, no expertise, no rapport, no sales career. The business can never scale if it's built on reps who communicate like every other average salesperson.

WHAT HIGH-LEVEL COMMUNICATION MEANS:

1. You talk with impact
2. You do not sound salesy
3. You sound authentic
4. You have eliminated unintentional mistakes from your language

ELIMINATE FILLER WORDS:
Um. Ah. Like. You know. Any word or sound coming out of your mouth that isn't intentional.

If you're speaking to a customer and you're stumbling over every sentence - um, uh, come on - you will not earn one ounce of respect. You will not be perceived as an expert in your field. Full stop.

HOW TO FIX IT:
Train every single day. Catch yourself every time you stutter, every time you say um or ah. Make it a conscious habit. It won't happen overnight but it compounds quickly once you start actively working on it.

THE ADVANCED MOVE - INTENTIONAL PAUSING:
Once you've eliminated unintentional filler words, you can start using pauses and hesitations intentionally as a tool.

Example: You've been speaking fluidly and confidently throughout the call. Now you want the customer to really lean in and pay attention to something important. You slow down, drop a deliberate "and... uh..." before the key question or statement.

What happens? Their attention spikes. Their retention increases. The contrast between your fluid speech and the deliberate pause signals: this matters.

You cannot do this effectively unless you've first eliminated the unintentional version. When you stutter all the time, a pause means nothing. When you almost never stutter, a pause means everything.

BASICS CHECKLIST:
- Speak clearly - customer must be able to understand you
- No muffled voice
- No unintentional um/ah/like/you know
- Speak fluidly and confidently
- Eventually learn to use pauses as an intentional tool

This is the foundation. No other sales skill works without this underneath it.
