OBJECTION PREVENTION
Source: Sales Basics Module

The entire 12-pillar presentation is built on this principle. Objection prevention is not a separate technique - it's the underlying theme of the whole script.

THE CORE RULE:
Always easier to prevent an objection before the price is revealed than to handle it after.

If you get to the end of the call with unhandled objections - you won't get the sale. If you've prevented those objections throughout the presentation - your close rate multiplies.

HOW IT WORKS IN PRACTICE:

PREVENTING "I want to look at other companies / get more quotes":

After showing the numbers look better than their current bill, say:
"Guys - this is why it literally baffles me that anyone would even roll the dice with one of those cheap companies. You know, with the fire risks and everything that could happen - when the numbers look this good with the best company in [location] that actually cares about you and has all these warranties protecting you... it just baffles me that that would even be a route some people take. And I'm not saying that because I think you guys would do that - not at all. It just honestly shocks me."

What you've done: made it feel irrational and almost embarrassing to go look at cheaper companies. They now associate "shopping around" with being the kind of person who makes bad decisions. They don't want to be that person.

PREVENTING "The price is too high":

In Pillar 3, before any price is mentioned:
"I'm going to be very transparent with you - we're not the dirt cheapest in the world. That's never going to be us."

What you've done: when the price comes up later and it's expensive, they're not shocked. They expected it. Either they think "yeah, that's what he said" (fine) or it's slightly less than they imagined (even better). There is no downside to telling someone a price will be high before they see it.

THE PRINCIPLE:
Pre-handling = plant the seed early so that by the time the objection would naturally arise, the prospect has already resolved it in their own mind.

Every pillar pre-handles something:
- Pillar 3 pre-handles: cheap companies, fire risk, price objection, "I want to shop around"
- Pillar 4 pre-handles: "will you be there after install?", "will the savings actually materialise?"
- Pillar 7 pre-handles: "what if something goes wrong?", "what if you go out of business?"
- Pillar 10 pre-handles: "I want to do more research", price pushback, comparison shopping

Be conscious of this as you execute each pillar. Know which objection each section is pre-handling. The more intentionally you execute the prevention, the fewer objections you face at the close.
