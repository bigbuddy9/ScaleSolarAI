THE OFFER
Source: Sales Basics Module

"Make offers so good people feel stupid saying no." - Alex Hormozi

If your offer is wrong, nothing else matters. This is the spine of your business. Get this right and everything becomes more fluid, more cohesive, easier to scale.

THE FOUNDATIONAL NO-BRAINER:
You can go solar for less than your current bill. That is the underlying no-brainer. It lands because they're already paying that money - you're just redirecting it to something they own. Like upgrading from daily Ubers to a Rolls Royce picking you up every day for the same cost, and after 10 years you own the Rolls Royce.

This gets layered with 6 levers to make it a complete no-brainer offer.

THE 6 LEVERS:

LEVER 1 - Payments Same or Lower Than Current Bill (the biggest one):
There is absolutely no way it doesn't make sense when they're already paying this money. This is the foundation everything else is built on. If the repayment is less than their current power bill, the offer is fundamentally a no-brainer before you even add anything else.

LEVER 2 - Fully Guaranteed, Zero Risk (the warranties):
Build a comprehensive list of 6 guarantees/warranties. These cost almost nothing extra on the backend but completely transform how the offer feels.

The 6 warranties:
1. X-Day Downtime Guarantee - pick a timeframe you can confidently hit (5, 10, 15 days). If the system goes down, you're out to fix it within X days. You should be doing this anyway - this just frames it as a guarantee.

2. 100% Savings Guarantee - leverage the panel manufacturer's performance warranty. Longi guarantees 88.9% output after 25 years. All your savings calculations are based on that rated output. So: if panels fall below rated output, you cover the savings difference. The actual failure rate is extremely low, but the customer feels completely protected.

3. Active Monitoring Service - you have third-party monitoring through the inverter. Nothing special on the backend. Just frame it as an active monitoring service where you know before they do if anything goes wrong.

4. Hail Damage Cover - leverage the panel's hail warranty. As long as the hailstone is within what the panel is rated for, you cover it. The panel manufacturer is actually picking it up. Frame it as your guarantee.

5. Parts and Labour Cover - if installers cause any damage on the install, you fix it. Your installers shouldn't be causing damage anyway - this just gives the customer peace of mind.

6. Ongoing Savings Optimisation - when a better energy retailer becomes available, you notify your customers. You have their data. A simple message to them keeps them on the best rate. Costs you almost nothing. Frames as an ongoing service commitment.

These 6 warranties transform the offer from "a solar investment" to "a no-brainer that protects me in every single way and makes me money no matter what happens."

LEVER 3 - Locked Payments:
The green loan repayment does not skyrocket every month like power bills do. Reiterate this clearly. After the loan term (5, 10, 15 years) - no more payments at all. Future pain from rising bills completely disappears. This is extremely powerful for prospects who understand compounding bill increases.

LEVER 4 - Energy Independence:
More cyclones, more natural disasters, more grid instability over time. With solar + battery, they're completely protected from blackouts. They have their own energy. Situational - some people love this more than others. Read the room.

LEVER 5 - Property Value Add:
Solar and battery adds significant value to the home (reference local property data - e.g. 14% increase in Australia). Lean into this harder if they plan to sell in 5 years. If they're staying for 25 years, touch on it but don't over-index.

LEVER 6 - Better for the Environment:
Pick your audience. If the customer clearly cares about the environment - hit this lever hard. If they don't - touch on it briefly and move on. Don't waste time on this with people who clearly aren't motivated by it.

THE COST OF WAITING:
Every single month they wait, they're paying a power bill that could have been going toward owning a system. Once they get their head around this, it becomes illogical to wait. Use this to create genuine urgency - not fake pressure, just the logical reality.

PERSONALISATION (critical):
People pay a premium for customised, specific solutions. They don't care about a generic solar system copy-pasted to 20 other people.

Frame everything as specifically tailored for them:
- Their usage
- Their roof
- Their bill
- Their property
- Their situation

"Our technicians designed this specifically for you." This is how Resync charged 40-60% more than competitors and still closed at high rates. People are happy to pay a premium when it's a solution built for them specifically.

WHY THIS MATTERS BEYOND THE CUSTOMER:
When your offer is genuinely a no-brainer, your entire team sells with more conviction. Door knockers, cold callers, closers, admins - everyone believes in it. That belief compounds through every conversation. The whole business becomes more effective when the offer is actually undeniable.

If you copy and paste the same generic solar system as everyone else, you're the same as everyone else. Frame it this way and you're in a different category entirely.

Make offers so good people feel stupid saying no. That's it.
