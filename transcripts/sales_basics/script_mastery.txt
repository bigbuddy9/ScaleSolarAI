SCRIPT MASTERY
Source: Sales Basics Module

If you don't have the script memorised, you are at a severe disadvantage. If you're reading from a script or blanking on what comes next during a presentation, you've lost the sale before it starts.

WHY IT MATTERS:
When your mental energy goes toward remembering the script, you have nothing left to actually run the sale. Once the script is locked in, your full attention goes to:
- Listening to the customer
- Reading their body language and energy
- Noticing when their attention drops
- Hearing their tonality and concerns
- Catching objections before they form
- Building genuine rapport in real time

Script mastery is what frees you to be a real salesperson instead of a presenter reading from a document.

HOW TO GET THERE:

1. Write out the key points of every section - not word for word, but the key statements you want to land exactly right. The phrases that hit hard. The lines that move people.

2. Read it daily. The full script is long - at minimum, read your key points every single day.

3. Memorise the exact verbiage for the lines that matter most. Some lines are too important to paraphrase. Know those cold.

4. Test yourself front-to-back and back-to-front. Can you walk through the entire presentation from memory, start to finish? Can you go backwards? If yes, you know it.

5. The 3am test: could you wake up at 3am and recite every part of the script perfectly? That's the standard.

THE GOAL:
The script should be so locked in that you never think about it during a call. You're just present with the customer, using the script as an invisible backbone that guides the conversation without ever being visible.

Know it 100%. No exceptions.
