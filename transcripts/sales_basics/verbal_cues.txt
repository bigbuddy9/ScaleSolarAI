VERBAL CUES
Source: Sales Basics Module

Simple to implement. Immediately makes you a better sales rep.

WHAT THEY ARE:
Small sounds and words you use while the customer is speaking to show you're listening.
Examples: "Mhm", "Sure", "Okay", "Alright", "Makes sense", "Yep", "I get you"

HOW TO USE THEM:
Write down 5-7 verbal cues that feel natural to you. Loop them throughout the call whenever the customer is speaking. Use them sparingly - not after every word - but consistently enough that the customer feels heard.

WHY THEY MATTER:

1. Shows you're listening
When someone is talking and you're giving verbal cues, they feel genuinely heard. It makes the conversation feel like a real dialogue, not a sales pitch where someone is just waiting to talk again.

2. Makes cutting in smooth
If the customer is going down a thought pattern that's heading toward a bigger objection or a route that doesn't benefit you - verbal cues let you cut in naturally:
"Mhm, sure, yeah - wait, actually, what about..."
vs. sitting in complete silence and then abruptly interrupting. The cue creates a natural opening to redirect.

3. Lets you catch objections early
When you're actively listening and cuing, you catch where their head is going. You can address a concern before it becomes fully formed and entrenched in their mind.

4. Makes the conversation more natural
Sales calls that feel like conversations close better than sales calls that feel like presentations. Verbal cues are one of the easiest ways to shift the dynamic from pitch → dialogue.

ACTION:
Write down your 7 verbal cues now. Use them on your next call. It's that simple.
